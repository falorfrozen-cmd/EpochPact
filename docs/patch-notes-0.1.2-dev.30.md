# EpochPact 0.1.2-dev.30

More minions, totems, ballistas, forged weapons and companions — and minions
that do not die.

## Summons → Minion counts

- **Extra minions per skill** (0–20): raises each summon skill's own limit —
  skeletons, skeletal mages, wraiths, golems, zombies and other minions. Cast the
  skill again to fill the new places.
- **Extra totems per skill** (0–10), **Extra ballistas** (0–10) and **Extra
  forged weapons** (0–20): the same for totems, ballistas and forged weapons.
- **Extra companions** (0–10): raises your companion limit. The game's other
  companion rules stay, such as one of each kind.
- **Immortal minions:** your minions, companions, totems and ballistas cannot be
  killed by damage — lethal damage leaves them at 0 health. They still end when
  their duration runs out or when they are unsummoned. Switching it off gives a
  minion at 0 health 1 health back.
- Like the other session settings, these are applied again when your character
  is ready and end when you close EpochPact.

Skills or passives that consume minions through damage may not work while
**Immortal minions** is on. Many more minions also mean more work for the game.

## Translations

- The new texts are translated into Türkçe and Русский.

## Updating

With 0.1.2-dev.22 or later: **New version · What's new → Install update** in
the app. From an older version: close Last Epoch and EpochPact, extract the
**complete new ZIP**, open `EpochPact/EpochPact.exe`, and select **Game setup →
Reinstall / update mod**. The new settings need the updated mod: Install update
reinstalls it once the game is closed.
